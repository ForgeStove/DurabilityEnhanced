# Durability Enhanced

## 项目简介

> **Durability Enhanced** 是一个仅服务端的模组，旨在优化铁砧修复机制，提升游戏体验。

- **核心功能**：允许玩家在铁砧修复物品时，不再累计修复惩罚（即修复成本不会随着修复次数增加而递增），让装备的使用寿命大幅提升。

## 为什么选择 Durability Enhanced？
- 解决了原版铁砧修复成本过高、装备易报废的问题，极大提升了装备的可持续利用性。
- 适合追求长期生存、装备养成、RPG 玩法等服务器，增强玩家粘性和游戏乐趣。
- 配置简单，易于上手，支持多语言（中英）。

## 功能一览
- 铁砧修复装备时不再增加惩罚（可选）。
- 支持 NeoForge 及 Cloth Config 配置界面。
- 兼容 Minecraft 1.21.1 及后续版本。

> **Durability Enhanced** is a server-side mod for Minecraft that optimizes the anvil repair mechanism, making item repairs no longer accumulate penalties. This greatly extends the lifespan of your equipment and enhances the gameplay experience.

- **Core Feature**: Prevents the anvil from increasing repair costs with each use (no repair penalty accumulation).

**Why use Durability Enhanced?**
- Solves the problem of high repair costs and short equipment lifespan in vanilla Minecraft.
- Perfect for long-term survival, RPG, and progression servers.
- Simple setup, multi-language support (EN/CH).

**Features:**
- No repair penalty accumulation (optional).
- NeoForge & Cloth Config GUI support.
- Compatible with Minecraft 1.21.1 and above.
